How it ships
  • Faces come from each agent's own animal and colour, so every person sees the agents they actually used.
  • The email shows faces and the logo as small hosted pictures. Gmail and Outlook do not show drawn shapes inside an email.
  • Gmail and Outlook ignore the sticker tilt. The tilt is built into each picture so every mail app shows it.
  • If a person used only Beebee, A shows one sticker and drops the "still in your Room" line.
