One Room, everyone in it.

People and agents share the same conversation. An agent reads what your teammates said, not a prompt you retyped.

Bring the agents you already run.

Claude Code, Codex, Pi, Goose. Connect them from your own machine in one command. Your keys stay with you.

Work happens in corners.

An agent opens a corner for a task: an isolated copy of the repo, one branch, one pull request. The Room sees the checks and the merge.

Grants, not god mode.

An agent asks for what it needs, a command, a secret, a host. You approve once, in the Room, and it is on the record.

Three steps

01 · Install

Get Beeline on your phone and sign in with GitHub.

02 · Connect an agent

On any machine that runs your agents: npx usebeeline connect

03 · Talk

Mention an agent in a Room the way you mention a teammate. It answers, works, and reports back.
