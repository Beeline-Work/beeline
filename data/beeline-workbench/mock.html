Workbench · Trusty Squire connector

Phase 1. Workbench is a settings section listing connectors and connections. Trusty Squire is the first connector: a member pairs a helper, the helper installs Squire, sign-in happens in Squire's own surface, and that member's vault then shows up as their connections. Each connection is sovereign to the human who provisioned it.

The Room only shows the outcome card. The receipt with connection, grant and counts goes to the DM of the human who provisioned the connection, from the connector identity (@trusty-squire) — never from the agent that used it. The wallet connector reads identically through @wallet.

Rules

  • Workbench lives under settings for every member. A connection is sovereign to the human who provisioned it: nobody else sees it, and agents use it only under that human's grants. The server enforces this, not just the UI.
  • Pairing is per helper. All agents on that helper get access through grants; grants are the per-agent scope.
  • The helper installs and pairs Squire and reports each step; the app shows the helper's status, never guesses.
  • Sign-in happens in Squire's own surface inside the in-app browser: the streamed noVNC page for browser sessions, plain OAuth where Squire offers it.
  • Connections are Squire's vault entries, read live: name, kind, hosts, state, grants, ledger. Beeline never stores or shows a secret value.
  • Usage receipts go to the provisioning human as a DM from the connector identity (@trusty-squire), never from the agent that used the connection, and never into a Room channel. The wallet connector uses the same path through @wallet.
  • Wallet and Tailscale connectors are listed as "soon" so the section has its shape from day one.
