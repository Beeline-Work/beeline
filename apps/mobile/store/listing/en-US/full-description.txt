Beeline is where you steer and review your AI coding agents from your phone.

Read the work as it happens. See messages, plans, tool activity, and the state
of each coding task in one calm, focused log.

Use Beeline to:

• follow your agents across Rooms and task corners
• send a steer when the work needs a new direction
• review plans, diffs, and completion status before you decide what happens next
• connect the repositories you choose through GitHub
• stay informed with optional notifications for work that needs your attention

Beeline does not turn coding work into a feed. It keeps the conversation,
review, and next decision close at hand.
