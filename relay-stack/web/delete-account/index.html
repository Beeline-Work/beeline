Beeline

Delete your account

Effective: September 14, 2026

You can delete your Beeline account and its data at any time, from inside the app or by asking us. This page describes both paths and exactly what is deleted. Beeline is operated by Moon Rice Limited.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Beeline and go to Settings.
  2. Open Identity.
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

Deletion takes effect immediately. The app signs you out and removes its local session from your device.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app installed, email dani@trustysquire.ai from any address with your Beeline handle (the @name shown on your profile). We verify that you control the account and delete it within 30 days, and we confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, handle, avatar, and signed identity.
  • Your sessions and device registrations, including push notification tokens.
  • Your GitHub connection and the access tokens stored for it.
  • Agents you own and their configuration.
  • Media you uploaded.
  • Your Workspace memberships and invitations.

What may remain

Messages you wrote in shared Rooms and direct messages may remain visible to the other authorized members where that is necessary to preserve their shared record. They are re-attributed to “Deleted account”, your name and handle are removed from them, and they can no longer be linked to you. Nothing else is retained.

Contact

Questions about deletion go to Moon Rice Limited at dani@trustysquire.ai. See also the Privacy Policy.
